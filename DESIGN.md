# ForThink website

The light editorial layout and stepped motion draw on the Nexus GP reference. ForThink pairs a pale survey-blue canvas with a dark aerial site photograph. Thin blue and amber coverage marks explain the 360° product in a labeled illustration. ForThink’s own blue mark anchors the header and product pages.

The site opens with the construction safety offer, then moves through devices, EHIGH positioning, software services, Hong Kong customer logos and direct enquiry. The six solution pages extend the same visual system with their own images, specifications and contact actions. Headings use Barlow Semi Condensed. Body text uses Inter with system Chinese fallbacks.

Controls use readable labels and visible keyboard focus. The illustration changes on request and respects reduced motion. The project references and contact details remain readable without JavaScript. Generated construction scenes illustrate use cases. The positioning hardware images come from slide 12 of the company deck.

The blue line below the header tracks page progress. The aerial image moves with scroll and the product sections rise into view. A single queued animation frame handles scroll progress and parallax. An intersection observer starts the section transitions where supported. Reduced-motion users and visitors with JavaScript disabled get a static presentation.
