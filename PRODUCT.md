# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The local Docker Desktop deployment uses Caddy and the container name `4T-ComWebsite`.

## Users

Assumed primary audience: Hong Kong contractors, safety managers and project teams evaluating site safety equipment and positioning systems.

## Product purpose

Help a buyer identify suitable hardware, understand its application and ask ForThink about deployment on their site.

## Positioning

ForThink (HK) Technology Co., Limited (四相致新（香港）科技有限公司) offers smart site safety hardware, custom software and AI consultation in Hong Kong. EHIGH 恒高 (Everhigh) is ForThink’s positioning technology brand.

## Capabilities and constraints

4S devices include a smart watch, smart lock, MEWP sensor for scissor lifts and 360° Mobile Plant Anti-Collision System. The positioning catalogue includes EHIGH UWB tags and anchors, hybrid UWB and Bluetooth systems, EmapEng, Etrack, Ecloud and forklift anti-collision. Product specifications and approvals are model-specific.

## Brand commitments

Use the supplied ForThink bilingual mark. The brief calls for a contemporary technology feel. Traditional Chinese is the default, with English and Simplified Chinese versions.
